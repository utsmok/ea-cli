Explanation of columns:

            - Faculty: the abbreviation of the faculty -- all data is per faculty
            - Probable fine: the sum of all fines for items that are manually classified as 'lange overname'
            - Max fine: the sum of all fines for all items except those manually classified as 'eigen materiaal' or 
'open access'
            - Items total: the total number of items selected by the 'CopyRight tool' (i.e. all pdfs with 40+ pages)
            - Infringements: the number of items that are manually classified as 'lange overname' -- plus as a 
percentage of total number of items
            - Non-infringements: the number of items manually classified as 'eigen materiaal' or 'open access' -- plus 
as a percentage of total number of items
            - To be classified: the number of items that are not yet manually classified -- plus as a percentage of 
total number of items
